# Global Shipping & Logistics Operations Manual

## 1. Domestic Shipping Timelines & Rates
We partner with leading courier carriers (BlueDart, Delhivery, FedEx, IndiaPost) to deliver orders across 25,000+ pin codes.

- **Standard Delivery (Free on orders above ₹999):** Delivered within 3 to 5 business days for major metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata). Non-metro and tier-2/tier-3 cities require 5 to 7 business days.
- **Express Air Shipping (Flat ₹250):** Delivered within 24 to 48 hours for eligible metro pin codes if placed before 2:00 PM IST.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Available for orders under ₹10,000. A nominal COD handling fee of ₹50 applies at checkout.

## 2. Order Tracking & Dispatch Alerts
As soon as your order is packed and dispatched from our warehouse, you will receive an automated SMS, WhatsApp alert, and Email containing:
- Direct Courier AWB Tracking Link.
- Estimated Date of Delivery (EDD).
- Live Driver Contact Info on the morning of delivery.

If your shipment is delayed past the EDD due to weather or transit disruptions, our Logistics Team proactively monitors and pushes courier escalations every 12 hours.

## 3. Package Damage, Missing Items & Theft Protection
All shipments are 100% insured against loss or damage in transit.
- **Tampered Boxes:** If the courier box arrives taped over, crushed, or open, DO NOT accept the package from the delivery agent. Mark it as "Refused due to damage" on the POD sheet.
- **Unboxing Video Policy:** For high-value electronics (Laptops, Smartphones above ₹20,000), customers are required to record a continuous, unedited unboxing video showing the outer shipping label and opening of seal. This guarantees 100% instant refund/replacement if an item is missing or physically cracked upon arrival.
