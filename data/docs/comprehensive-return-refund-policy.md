# Comprehensive Return, Exchange & Refund Policy

## 1. Overview & General Policy Terms
At Afzal Store, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We offer a comprehensive 30-day money-back guarantee for most physical products purchased through our official website or mobile app. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may initiate a return or exchange request within 30 calendar days from the date of package delivery.

All returned items must be in their original, unused condition, with all original tags, labels, protective films, accessories, manuals, and packaging intact. Products showing signs of wear, physical damage, altered software/firmware, or missing original accessories will be rejected or subjected to a 20% restocking fee.

## 2. Apparel, Footwear & Accessories Return Rules
- **Apparel (Shirts, Jackets, Jeans):** Must be unworn, unwashed, and free of odors, pet hair, or stains. Hygiene seals must not be removed.
- **Footwear (Sneakers, Shoes):** Must be tried on indoors on carpeted surfaces. Shoes showing scuffs on outsoles will not be accepted for full refund.
- **Underwear, Swimwear & Socks:** Due to health and hygiene regulations, these items are strictly final sale and non-returnable unless defective.

## 3. Electronics & Tech Products Return Rules
- **Laptops, Smartphones & Tablets:** Must be factory reset to original state. All personal data, Google Accounts, iCloud accounts, and screen locks must be completely removed prior to shipping back. Locked devices cannot be processed.
- **Defective Electronics:** Items experiencing hardware defects within 14 days of delivery qualify for an immediate, free replacement or full refund. After 14 days, issues are covered under the standard manufacturer warranty.

## 4. Refund Processing Timelines & Bank Methods
Once your return shipment reaches our central fulfillment warehouse, our Quality Inspection team verifies the package within 48 business hours. 
- **Credit / Debit Cards:** Refunds are issued back to the original payment card within 3-5 business days depending on your issuing bank.
- **Net Banking & UPI:** Refunds are processed within 24-48 hours.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD):** COD orders are refunded via direct bank NEFT transfer or Store Credit Voucher within 3 business days of bank details confirmation.
- **Return Shipping Fee:** Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items sent by us. For standard change-of-mind returns, a flat ₹150 return processing fee will be deducted from your total refund amount.
