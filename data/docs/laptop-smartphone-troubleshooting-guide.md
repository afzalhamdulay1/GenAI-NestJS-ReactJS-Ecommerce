# Laptop & Smartphone Technical Support & Troubleshooting Guide

## 1. Laptop Display & Boot Failure Solutions
If your newly purchased laptop fails to turn on or displays a blank black screen:
- **Hard Reset Procedure:** Disconnect the AC power adapter. Hold down the Power Button continuously for 30 seconds to drain residual motherboard capacitor energy. Reconnect the charger and power on normally.
- **External Display Check:** Plug an HDMI cable into an external TV or monitor. If video outputs to the TV, the laptop screen or ribbon cable requires replacement under warranty.
- **RAM Reseating:** If the power LED blinks in a 2-blink or 3-blink pattern, the RAM sticks may have dislodged during courier shipping. Contact support for authorized technician guidance.

## 2. Smartphone Battery Drain & Heating Solutions
- **Battery Calibration:** Charge your device to 100%, leave it plugged in for 2 extra hours, drain it to 0% until it shuts down, and recharge to 100% uninterrupted.
- **Overheating Warnings:** Devices running high-end games or 4K video recording outdoors in 40°C heat will naturally warm up to 43°C. The internal thermal protection system automatically reduces processor clock speeds to protect battery health.
- **Factory Software Reset:** If battery drains over 15% overnight while idle, clear cache partition from Recovery Mode or perform a Backup & Factory Data Reset.
