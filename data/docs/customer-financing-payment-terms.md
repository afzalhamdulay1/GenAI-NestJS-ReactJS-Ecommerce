# Customer Financing, EMI & Payment Terms

## 1. Supported Payment Gateways & Credit Cards
We accept 100% secure payments via Stripe, Razorpay, and PayPal. 
- **Debit / Credit Cards:** Visa, MasterCard, American Express, RuPay, Maestro.
- **Digital Wallets:** Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, Amazon Pay, Apple Pay.
- **Net Banking:** Supported across 55+ Indian banks including HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, and Kotak.

## 2. No-Cost EMI & Credit Card EMI Options
- **No-Cost EMI (3 to 6 Months):** Available on purchases above ₹5,000 using leading bank credit cards (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis). The interest charged by the bank is upfront discounted from the product price.
- **Low-Cost EMI (9 to 24 Months):** Flexible monthly installments at standard bank interest rates (13% - 15% p.a.).

## 3. Failed Transactions & Double Deductions
If money is debited from your bank account or card but your order status shows "Payment Pending" or "Failed":
- Do not panic! This happens during bank gateway timeouts.
- The debited amount is automatically held in bank escrow and will automatically reverse to your card within 48 to 72 business hours.
- If your order fails 3 times consecutively, clear your browser cookies or try paying via UPI QR code.
