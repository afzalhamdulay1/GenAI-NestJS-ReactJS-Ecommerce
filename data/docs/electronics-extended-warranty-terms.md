# Electronics & Appliance Extended Warranty Framework

## 1. Standard Brand Warranty Overview
All brand-new electronic devices (Smartphones, Laptops, Audio Gear, Wearables) sold on Afzal Store carry a minimum 1-Year Manufacturer Warranty starting from the tax invoice date.

- **Brand Service Centers:** For major brands (Apple, Asus, Samsung, OnePlus, Sony), warranty claims can be processed directly at any authorized service center nationwide using your electronic tax invoice downloaded from your My Account portal.

## 2. Afzal Care+ Extended Warranty & Damage Protection
When purchasing laptops or smartphones, customers can opt for **Afzal Care+** extended warranty cover:
- **1-Year Extended Warranty:** Extends hardware malfunction coverage for an additional 12 months after the manufacturer warranty expires.
- **Accidental & Liquid Damage Protection (ADLD):** Covers screen cracks, liquid spills, submersion, and accidental drops for up to 80% of the device invoice value.

## 3. How to File a Warranty Claim
1. Log into your account and navigate to **My Orders -> Claim Warranty**.
2. Select your device and upload a photo/video describing the defect.
3. Choose **Doorstep Pickup Service** (available in 150+ cities) or **Self-Shipment**.
4. Our repair team diagnoses the device within 72 hours. If repair takes longer than 7 business days, a free temporary standby device is provided.
5. If the motherboard or display cannot be repaired, a 100% full replacement device or equivalent Store Credit is issued.
